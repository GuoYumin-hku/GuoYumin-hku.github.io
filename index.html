About关于

I am a Ph.D. student at The University of Hong Kong, supervised by Prof. Kai Han. My work starts from visual perception and moves toward models that can both understand and act: multimodal large language models, vision-language-action architectures, and agents that improve themselves from their own experience.

Before starting the Ph.D. I completed an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence at HKU and spent two years as an algorithm engineer at Onewo (Vanke Service), where I built detection systems serving hundreds of thousands of cameras across 30+ cities. I care most about problems where data and evaluation — not only architecture — decide the outcome; that concern led to a 1st-place finish at the CVPR 2024 DataCV Challenge.

I am open to collaboration on multimodal model evaluation, embodied agents, and data-centric vision.

Research Experience科研经历

Exploring Self-Evolving Methods for Multi-Agent Frameworks 多智能体框架的自进化方法研究

2025.07 – 2025.12

M.Sc. Graduation Project · Team Leader · HKU, supervised by Prof. Kai Han 硕士毕业设计 · 团队负责人 · 香港大学，韩锴教授指导

  • Designed three complementary training-free self-evolution mechanisms: prompt-based strategy evolution, memory-enhanced retention across long interactions, and majority-vote stabilisation of critical decisions.
  • Evaluated on the MARBLE benchmark — collaborative coding, adversarial Werewolf (9 agents), and bilateral bargaining — across 7+ LLMs including GPT-5-mini, GPT-4.1-mini, DeepSeek-V3.2, Qwen-3-Max, Gemini-2.5-Flash and Llama-3.3-70B.
  • Results: +8.5% coding-agent KPI, villager win rate 60% → 80%, bargaining rounds 14.3 → 5.3 (−62%); GPT-4.1-mini more than tripled its win rate (20% → 60%), letting smaller models approach stronger ones at zero training cost.
  • Ablations isolate the contribution of each component and show the combination consistently outperforms any single strategy.
  • 设计三种互补的免训练自进化机制：基于提示词的经验驱动策略演化、长程交互中的记忆增强保留、以及以多数投票稳定关键决策。
  • 在 MARBLE 基准上评测——多智能体协同编码、9 智能体狼人杀对抗、双边议价——覆盖 GPT-5-mini、GPT-4.1-mini、DeepSeek-V3.2、Qwen-3-Max、Gemini-2.5-Flash、Llama-3.3-70B 等 7+ 个模型。
  • 结果：编码智能体 KPI +8.5%，村民胜率 60% → 80%，议价轮数 14.3 → 5.3（−62%）；GPT-4.1-mini 胜率由 20% 提升至 60%，使小模型在零训练成本下逼近更强模型。
  • 消融实验分离各组件贡献，并验证组合方法稳定优于任一单一策略。
